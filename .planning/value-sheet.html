How each value figure is built

Risk-shielded (defect cost × probability)

value_shielded = P(incident in yr 1) × cost_per_incident × incidents_prevented

Industry baseline: a production defect that reaches users costs ~100× more to fix than the same defect caught at commit time (IBM Systems Sciences Institute). A payment-flow incident at a paid-membership platform our size costs an estimated $3,000–$8,000 in lost transactions, support time, and refund processing per occurrence. Smoke + CI + boundaries each catch a different incident class, so values are summed, not intersected.

Growth-enabled (conversion / retention lift)

value_enabled = members × ARPU × lift_basis_points

Graceful error UX on checkout is documented to recover 1–3% of failed transactions (Stripe + Baymard Institute findings). For a $99/yr Bronze tier at the projected year-one member count, a 1.5% conversion recovery alone covers the entire engineering spend.

Compliance-enabled (insurance + audit savings)

value = annual_premium × hardening_discount + audit_hours_saved × audit_rate

Cyber-liability premiums drop materially when the insurer's questionnaire returns "yes" on signed webhooks, rate limits, security headers, dependency scanning, and CODEOWNERS-enforced review. For a small SaaS, that is typically a 10–20% premium discount. Future SOC 2 Type I prep is also ~20 hours cheaper when these controls already exist.

Ops-enabled (engineering throughput)

value = engineers × hours_saved_per_week × rate × 50_weeks

Pre-commit hooks and lint baselines remove ~30 minutes per developer per week of formatting churn and CI round-trips. Across a two-developer team for one year, that's roughly 50 hours recovered.

What "shielded" actually means

None of these figures are revenue. They are the dollar amount of future loss avoided or future income preserved, probability-weighted to the realistic year-one footprint of the platform. They become more conservative if member growth lags, and more generous if it accelerates — the framework scales with the business.

Methodology + sources

  • Defect cost multiplier — IBM Systems Sciences Institute, "Relative Cost of Fixing Defects" (1× design → 100× production).
  • Cost of a production incident — Atlassian State of Incident Management; Gremlin Incident Cost Survey (small SaaS: $1k–$10k/incident depending on duration).
  • Checkout error-handling conversion lift — Baymard Institute "E-Commerce Checkout Usability"; Stripe Payments Report 2024 (1–3% recoverable on payment errors).
  • Cyber-liability premium impact — Coalition / Marsh "State of the Cyber Market" 2024 (10–25% premium variance based on security control posture).
  • MTTR with observability — Sentry, "State of Frontend Reliability" 2024 (median MTTR 4 hrs without observability vs ~25 min with).
  • Developer hourly opportunity cost — assumed blended $125/hr (US mid-level, fully loaded).

All forward-looking dollar amounts are estimates. They are intended to convey order-of-magnitude impact to non-technical stakeholders, not to serve as accounting numbers. Actual outcomes depend on member growth, incident frequency, and insurer behavior.
